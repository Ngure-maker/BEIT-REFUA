Our Programs

Care for today. Hope for tomorrow.

Beit-Refuah is in its early, foundational season. Here is an honest breakdown of what is running today, what is being built, and what we are praying toward.

Current Programs

Spiritual Support and Discipleship

We share the Gospel by building trust through genuine service and presence, in ways that are culturally sensitive and respectful.

Regular Bible studies help individuals and groups apply Scripture to daily life, and prayer runs through everything we do. We also train local believers to lead discipleship movements, multiplying faith communities from the ground up.

Farming God's Way

A biblically grounded, sustainable agriculture program that helps vulnerable households build food security and income.

It develops character, work ethic and stewardship rooted in a right relationship with God — training and walking with people rather than simply giving to them.

Programs in Development

Being built, carefully

Community Medical Clinic

Under construction, opening as a Level 2 facility offering preventive, promotive, curative, rehabilitative and palliative care.

Mental Health & Trauma Support

Christian counseling, trauma healing, group therapy and pastoral care. We are seeking the right trained people to launch it responsibly.

Protection of Women and Children

Transitional support, psychosocial care and pathways toward vocational skills, built with organisations already experienced in this work.

Community Outreach & Evangelism

Home visits, relief support, prayer and practical assistance — the everyday, unglamorous work of showing up for people.

Future Vision
